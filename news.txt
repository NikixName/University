Breaking News: Scientists Discover New Species of Deep-Sea Fish

A team of marine biologists from the National Oceanic Research Institute has announced
the discovery of a previously unknown species of deep-sea fish living at depths of over
4,000 meters in the Pacific Ocean. The creature, tentatively named "Luminaris abyssalis,"
possesses bioluminescent organs along its entire body, producing a soft blue glow that
researchers believe may serve as both a mating signal and a predator deterrent.
